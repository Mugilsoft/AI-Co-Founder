# Responsible AI Principles — Reference Document

**Mugil Soft Solutions — Knowledge Base**

> This document serves as the authoritative reference for Mugil Soft Solutions' Responsible AI principles. It is designed for use in the RAG knowledge base to ground AI-assisted governance responses.

---

## Core Principles

Mugil Soft Solutions adopts five Responsible AI principles as its default governance framework:

1. **Fairness** — AI systems produce equitable outcomes and do not discriminate
2. **Transparency** — Users understand when and how AI is being used
3. **Accountability** — Clear ownership exists for AI systems and their outcomes
4. **Privacy** — Personal data is appropriately protected
5. **Human Oversight** — Humans can review, override, or intervene in AI decisions

---

## Application Rules

- These principles apply to all AI systems: internal, client, third-party, and consulting
- Every significant AI project is evaluated against all five principles before deployment
- The principles are operational requirements, not aspirational goals
- No AI system should be deployed with an unresolved critical issue against any principle
- Where principles conflict (e.g., transparency vs. privacy), document the trade-off and escalate to the founder

---

## Evaluation Questions

For each AI system, answer:

### Fairness
- Does this system create unfair outcomes for any group?
- Is the training data representative?
- Has disparate impact been tested?
- Are evaluation metrics disaggregated by relevant demographics?

### Transparency
- Do users know AI is being used?
- Can the AI's decisions be explained?
- Are limitations clearly communicated?
- Is documentation accessible?

### Accountability
- Who owns this AI system?
- Who is answerable for its decisions?
- Is there an incident reporting process?
- Are decisions auditable?

### Privacy
- What personal data is processed?
- Is data minimisation applied?
- Is there a lawful basis for processing?
- Are data subject rights supported?

### Human Oversight
- Can humans review AI outputs?
- Can humans override AI decisions?
- Is there an escalation path?
- Can affected people appeal?
