## 1. Executive Summary & Clinical Intent

This document establishes the mandatory **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** architecture and governance controls for Mugil Soft Solutions' **AI Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS)** and **Patient Record Analytics Platform** deployed in partner medical clinics.

### Non-Negotiable Medical Principle:
> **The AI system is strictly an assistive diagnostic tool and shall NEVER operate as an autonomous diagnostician or prescriber.**  
> Every diagnostic hypothesis, risk stratification score, and treatment suggestion generated by the AI is advisory and requires the explicit, documented validation of a registered attending physician prior to patient communication or clinical action.

## 4. Stage-by-Stage Specification

### Stage 1: Data Ingestion & Privacy Guardrails
- **Inputs**: Patient electronic health record (EHR), clinical notes, vitals telemetry, and lab test results.
- **Privacy Enforcement**:
  - Direct identifiers (Name, Phone, Aadhaar, Address) are redacted and replaced with a localized pseudonymized clinical ID.
  - Data transmission occurs over TLS 1.3; data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  - No patient data is retained by external LLM API providers for model training.

### Stage 2: Assisted Analysis & Explainability Engine
- **RAG Architecture**: The model references accredited clinical guidelines (e.g., ICMR clinical guidelines, WHO protocols, standard pharmacopoeia).
- **Required Output Schema**:
  1. **Primary Diagnostic Hypothesis** with quantitative confidence bracket (e.g., High, Moderate, Low).
  2. **Top 3 Differential Diagnoses** to guard against premature diagnostic closure.
  3. **Attribution & Evidence Links**: Explicit mapping linking each recommendation to specific elevated lab markers or symptoms.
  4. **Red Flag Alerts**: Highlighting urgent contraindications or potential emergency indicators.

### Stage 3: The Physician HITL Checkpoint (Core Guardrail)
The software user interface enforces the following controls to prevent **automation bias** (clinicians blindly accepting AI output):
1. **No Pre-Checked Confirmations**: The interface never defaults to "Accept".
2. **Dwell-Time Verification**: Enforces a minimum review threshold on multi-page diagnostic breakdowns to prevent rubber-stamping.
3. **Three Valid Action Paths**:
   - **Endorse & Adopt**: Physician agrees, confirms review of evidence, and signs cryptographically.
   - **Amend & Refine**: Physician adjusts the diagnosis or adjusts treatment parameters before signing.
   - **Complete Override**: Physician disagrees with AI reasoning and provides a one-sentence clinical rationale for rejection.

### Stage 4: Patient Delivery & Record Ingestion
- Only medical records carrying the verified electronic signature of the registered medical practitioner (RMP) are released to patient portals or pharmacy integrations.
- Disclaimers clearly document that diagnostic determinations are made by the attending physician.

### Stage 5: Discrepancy Logging & Model Monitoring
- Every instance of physician override or diagnostic dispute is logged anonymously in `memory/governance.md` and the AI Risk Register.
- If override rates for a specific diagnostic category exceed **15%**, an automatic alert is dispatched to the AI Lead for model recalibration and bias audit review.

## 5. Doctor Override & Anti-Automation Bias Protocol

To comply with **NIST AI RMF (Govern 1.2)** and **Mugil AI Principle 5**, clinicians retain complete sovereignty:

```
IF (Physician Diagnostic Assessment != AI Suggested Diagnosis):
    -> Physician Decision PREVAILS Instantly
    -> System Records Discrepancy Event:
        - Timestamp
        - Clinical Category (e.g., Cardiology, Endocrinology)
        - Anonymized Variance Summary
        - Physician Clinical Rationale
    -> System Flags Category for Quarterly Drift Review
```

---

## 6. Security & DPDP Compliance Summary

1. **Consent Management**: Patient digital consent is collected prior to processing records through the diagnostic support module.
2. **Data Minimization**: Only lab fields and clinical notes relevant to the current chief complaint are processed through the inference pipeline.
3. **Breach Notification Protocol**: Built-in alerting to notify Data Protection Officer within 72 hours in the event of any data integrity compromise.
