# Memory Store: Customer Interactions & Insights

## 1. Governance & Privacy Constraints
- **Privacy Rule**: No unmasked Personally Identifiable Information (PII), client credentials, or proprietary source code is retained in this store.
- **Purpose**: Record anonymized client feedback, industry vertical requirements, and project delivery learnings.

---

## 2. Target Industry Verticals & Requirements

### Healthcare Technology
- Key Requirements: DPDP Act compliance, patient data privacy, zero hallucination tolerance in medical data extraction, strict human-in-the-loop review.

### Enterprise ERP / CRM
- Key Requirements: High reliability, secure API integrations, role-based access control (RBAC), low latency.

### AI Consulting & Governance Audit
- Key Requirements: Formal risk reports, bias audit evidence, regulatory checklist mapping, clear executive presentation.
